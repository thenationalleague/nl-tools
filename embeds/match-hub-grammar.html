MATCH HUB · COMMENTARY GRAMMAR v0.25.0

Every phrase template, trigger condition and selection weight in the commentary engine, transcribed verbatim for critique. Lines marked with a green edge are new in v0.23. Tokens like {team} are filled at runtime; the crest and pins already carry the scoreline, so reveals prefer bare numbers ({scoreNum}) over repeating club names. {dp} renders "afternoon" or "evening" by the day's earliest kick-off.

What changed in v0.23

Change
✓Trajectory registers. Go-ahead splits into regain (led earlier, back in front) vs turnaround (trailed earlier, comeback complete). Pullback (one behind — game on) splits from consolation (two-plus still adrift). The 72nd-minute 2–1 case now reads "game on", never "consolation".
✓Shuffle-bag draw. Every pool is a shuffled deck drawn without replacement — no phrase repeats until its whole pool has been heard.
✓~45 new lines in the pools that fire most: openers 3→10, equalisers 3→8, extends 3→8, new regain/turnaround/pullback pools, wider timing windows, variant sets for the top-weighted storylines.
✓Truth-gates. The away-form claim now requires a genuine away drought (5+ winless on the road); the lead1 tease no longer assumes 1–0; own goals get timing drama; afternoon/evening decided by kick-off slot.
✓Dwell pacing. Every line earns a minimum on-screen time scaled to its length. Beats and reds queue behind the current read (reds > goals > beats) instead of overwriting it; kick-off previews pace at reading speed.
✓The console grows with the line (capped around a quarter of the viewport) instead of scrolling long sentences under the fold.

Try a situation

Set the score before the goal (from the scorer's point of view), the minute, and the flags — see how the engine classifies it and which grammar fires. The trajectory question only matters when the score was level (not 0–0).

How a goal call is assembled

A goal gets a two-beat treatment. First the tease — an intro naming the venue plus a question keyed to the match situation before the goal, while the map score is still hidden. Then the reveal — a phrase keyed to what this goal means, including the path the game took to get here — with a timing tag sometimes appended, and (55% of the time) one extra storyline sentence chosen by weighted lottery from every candidate whose condition is true. Every line then holds on screen for a dwell scaled to its length before anything may replace it.

✓ Fixed: "consolation" is no longer the fallback bucket. A goal that brings a side within one is a pullback — game-on register at any minute. Beats, cards & system lines

Beats and red cards now queue behind whatever is being read (reds outrank goals outrank beats) rather than overwriting it.

Kick-off

  • We're underway across the National League!
  • And they're off around the grounds.
  • Here we go — kick-off across the division!

Half-time

  • It's half-time.
  • The whistle goes for the break.
  • That's the first half done and dusted.

Second half

  • We're back underway.
  • Second halves are go across the grounds.
  • Off we go again — forty-five more.

Full-time

  • That's full-time across the board.
  • The final whistles are sounding.
  • And that's that — let's tot up the table.

Outro (wind-down after FT)

  • And that's your lot — here's how the table looks.
  • Full-time everywhere. Let's see the standings.
  • That's all she wrote — the final table.
  • The dust settles — this is how it finished.

  • {scorer} sees red for {team} at {venue}.
  • Trouble for {team} — {scorer} is dismissed at {venue}.
  • Off! {scorer} gets his marching orders at {venue}.

Second yellow

System

  • Team news is in — we're moments from kick-off across the National League. idle, before play

  • Back underway — picking up the action… on resume
  • Full-time across the division. seeked to the end while paused

Live day — additional grammar (v0.24–25)

Lines that only exist in live mode (the hub's default landing: today's games, wall-anchored).

Ghost goals — NLS score arrives before the scorer is entered
