# Wellbeing map

**Retired 28/11/2026.** Last commit holding it: the commit on `main` just before the one that removed it.

## What it was

A page at `/wellbeing-map/` that drew the public Wellbeing section as a map:
every page a box, every route an arrow, with the page's own words inside each
box. The people who look after the wellbeing content could move the boxes and
rewrite the words without touching code, then send the result back as one
file. It was a sketchpad. Nothing typed in it changed the live section;
changes were applied by hand.

It had no sign-in and stored nothing. Its two starting maps
(`map-live.json`, `map-concepts.json`) were made by
`scripts/build-wellbeing-map.js` from the old section's pages.

## Why it went

The section it mapped, `/wellbeing/`, was replaced by the Wellbeing Hub on
08/10/2026. From then the map showed a section that no longer existed, and
its build script refused to run. Marked Retire in `system/declutter.md` on
28/11/2026.

## If something like it is wanted again

- The idea that worked: people who own the words could reshape a section and
  hand back one file, with no login to think about.
- It would need to read the Hub, which is built differently from the old
  section, so it is a new reader, not a change of address.
- The Hub is generated by `scripts/build-wellbeing-hub.py`. A sketchpad that
  edits the content that script reads would be simpler than a map of the
  finished pages.
